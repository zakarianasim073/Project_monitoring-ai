## 2026-05-14 - [Login Accessibility and UX]
**Learning:** Standard login forms often overlook basic accessibility (linked labels) and simple UX conveniences (password visibility toggle), which are critical for user confidence and ease of entry.
**Action:** Always link form labels to inputs using `htmlFor`/`id` and provide a password visibility toggle with proper ARIA labels and focus indicators to ensure accessibility and reduce login frustration.
