## 2025-05-20 - [Enhanced Login Accessibility and UX]
**Learning:** Explicitly linking form labels to inputs using `htmlFor` and `id` is fundamental for accessibility. Providing interactive 'demo account' buttons that auto-populate forms significantly lowers onboarding friction compared to static text instructions.
**Action:** Always ensure form labels are correctly associated and consider adding pre-fill options for demo/test environments to improve the developer/reviewer experience.
