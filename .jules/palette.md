## 2025-05-19 - Login Accessibility & Interaction
**Learning:** Associating labels with inputs via `htmlFor/id` is often overlooked in demo-focused codebases but critical for screen readers. Adding a password toggle with `aria-label` and `focus-visible` styles significantly improves both security perception and accessibility.
**Action:** Always check form fields for missing labels and consider interaction feedback (like password visibility) as a standard 'Palette' micro-enhancement.
